# FAKHAMA – Luxury Arabian Perfume Brand Identity

FAKHAMA is a luxury perfume brand inspired by Arabian royalty and timeless sophistication. The name means grandeur, and every visual element was designed to reflect that essence. The brand targets modern women who value heritage, luxury, and exclusivity.

The concept merges Arabian warmth with European minimalism to feel premium in hand and iconic on shelf. The identity is built on two materials: deep burgundy velvet, representing passion and softness, and brushed gold foil, representing wealth and prestige.

The primary logo uses a high-contrast serif to convey heritage and trust. The secondary mark is a four-petal symbol inspired by Arabian floral motifs, used as a wax seal for instant recognition.

Packaging is designed as an experience. The outer box features soft-touch velvet lamination with gold foil borders and centered hot stamping. The bottle is heavy clear glass showcasing the amber juice, topped with a ribbed gold cap for tactile luxury. The display features a custom gold arch stand with a hanging F monogram, presenting the perfume like jewelry.

Modeled in Adobe Dimension with studio lighting, the mockups meet global luxury standards comparable to Dior and Jo Malone.

**Tools:** Adobe Illustrator, Dimension, Photoshop
